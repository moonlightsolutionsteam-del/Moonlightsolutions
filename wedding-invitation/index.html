Guest Guidelines

Good to Know

A few things that help both days run smoothly. Please share them with everyone in your family who is coming.

Before you come

RSVP is mandatory

Please RSVP on this page by 15 December 2026, so we can confirm final numbers with the resort and caterer. You will get an entry pass with a QR code. Save it on your phone and show it at the entrance.

Coming for one function only

Tick only what you will attend when you RSVP. For the Nikah, please reach the resort by 9:30 AM (seated by 9:50 AM); you are welcome to stay for lunch. For the Reception, please reach by 6:15 PM; the couple enters at 7:15 PM and dinner is from 8:00 PM.

Travel & parking

Please arrange your own travel to Greenwood Resort on Kanakapura Road. Carpool where you can; parking is available at the resort.

Bring a photo ID

Guests staying overnight should carry a government photo ID for resort check-in.

Who stays overnight

Rooms are assigned by the family. Rooms and cottages are shared equally between both families and allotted by name; we will confirm with you directly if you have one. Please do not book or request rooms at the resort yourself. Only guests on your family's room list can stay overnight; everyone else is welcome for the day.

January weather

Days are pleasant but mornings and evenings get cool. Pack a shawl or light jacket, comfortable footwear for lawns and stone paths, and swimwear if you plan to use the pool.

At the resort

Children

The resort has open lawns, steps, stone paths and a pool. Please keep children with a parent or guardian at all times. The resort and the host families are not responsible for accidents involving unsupervised children.

Pool safety

The pool is open only during the scheduled session on Day 1, at your own risk. No swimming after heavy meals, after dark, or outside the session.

Your belongings

Please look after your own cash, jewellery, phones and luggage, and lock your room whenever you step out. The resort and the host families are not responsible for loss or damage.

Health & medicines

Bring any regular medication. Tell your family coordinator in advance about allergies or dietary needs. Drinking water is set up around the lawn, and a first-aid kit is at the coordination desk.

Resort property

Please treat rooms, furniture and plants with care. Any damage charged by the resort is payable by the guest responsible. No smoking inside rooms or cottages.

During the celebrations

Please be on time

Food is cooked fresh to the schedule and headcount. Please be seated by 9:50 AM for the 10:00 AM Nikah.

Food

All food is halal and cooked on site. Vegetarian options are available on request through your family coordinator.

Photography

A professional photographer covers every function. During the Nikah, please stay seated and keep phones down so everyone can see.

Quiet hours

Please keep noise low after midnight on Wednesday so everyone is rested for the Nikah.

Checkout

Rooms and cottages must be vacated and keys returned by 10:00 PM on Thursday 21 January.

Harmony & respect

This is a celebration of two families becoming one. Please be patient and kind, and raise any concern quietly with your family coordinator, not in front of guests.
